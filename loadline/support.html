Support

Support opens when Loadline launches on the Atlassian Marketplace.

Getting started

After installing Loadline, a Jira admin opens Jira settings › Apps › Loadline. A setup checklist on that page walks through connecting Loadline to your Jira work, step by step.

Getting help

Raise a request in the Loadline Support portal. Anyone can sign up there. If you can't use the portal, email support@loadoutlabshq.com. We reply by the next business day to urgent problems and within 2 business days to questions; the support SLA has the hours, response times and what is covered.
