Getting started

Set up in Jira settings

After installing Loadline, a Jira admin opens Jira settings › Apps › Loadline. A setup checklist on that page walks through connecting Loadline to your Jira work, step by step.

Getting help

Two ways to reach us

Open a request

The Loadline Support portal is the quickest way to get help. Anyone can sign up, follow their request and reply in one place.

Loadline Support portal

Email support

If you can't use the portal, email us. Include your Jira site address, what you expected and what happened instead.
