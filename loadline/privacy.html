Privacy policy

What personal data Loadline handles, where it is kept and for how long.

Version 1.0, effective November 4, 2026

This policy explains what personal data the Loadline app for Jira Cloud ("Loadline") handles, where it is kept and for how long. Loadline is provided by Loadout Labs LLC, 4030 Wake Forest Rd Ste 349, Raleigh, NC 27609, USA ("Loadout Labs", "we").

Where Loadline runs

Loadline is built on Atlassian Forge and runs entirely on Atlassian's cloud infrastructure. Its data is kept in Atlassian-hosted storage (Forge SQL), in a separate database for each Jira site. Loadline sends no data to us or to any other service outside Atlassian. Atlassian's own privacy policy covers how Atlassian processes data on its platform.

Loadline also stores work data copied from Jira: issue keys, names, dates, statuses and team names, and progress totals for each epic (stories and sub-tasks done, story points, and hours logged and remaining, but not who logged them). This is not personal data, and it is refreshed at each sync. Daily plan snapshots are kept for 90 days; old ones are removed by the automatic sync, so on a site whose subscription has lapsed they are removed once it subscribes again.

Loadline does not store email addresses or passwords, and it never collects user credentials.

Who can see the data

Each person sees only work in Jira projects they can browse. Settings are visible only to Jira administrators. We have no access to any site's Loadline data.

The Rovo agent

When someone asks the Loadline agent a question in Rovo, it runs as that person and sees only what they can browse. Questions and answers stay within Atlassian.

Closed and changed accounts

Every week Loadline reports the account IDs it stores to Atlassian, as Atlassian's user privacy rules require.

  • Closed account: Loadline erases that person's account ID and name. Their history entries then show "Former user". A name typed in for a specialist without an account isn't linked to any account, so a Jira admin changes or removes it on Loadline's admin page.
  • Changed account: Loadline refreshes the stored display name.

Uninstalling and subscriptions

  • Lapsed subscription: Loadline keeps its data, so subscribing again restores everything.
  • Uninstall: Atlassian deletes the app's storage. Atlassian may keep it for up to 28 days in case the site asks for it to be recovered.

Data residency

Loadline's data follows the data residency of your Atlassian site, because it is kept only in Atlassian-hosted storage.

Your rights and contact

To exercise your data protection rights, contact your Jira site administrator, who controls Loadline on your site, or us at support@loadoutlabshq.com. Our data processing addendum is at https://loadoutlabshq.com/loadline/dpa.html.
