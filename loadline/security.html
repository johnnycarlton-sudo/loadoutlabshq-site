Security and privacy

Loadline runs entirely on Atlassian's cloud. Your plan never leaves Atlassian, and each person sees only what Jira already lets them see.

Where your data lives

Atlassian-hosted

Loadline is built on Atlassian Forge. Its data is in Atlassian-hosted storage, in a separate database for each Jira site, encrypted by the platform.

No data leaves Atlassian

Loadline calls no outside services and sends nothing to Loadout Labs. It qualifies for Atlassian's Runs on Atlassian programme.

Data residency

Loadline's data follows your site's data residency region, because it uses only Atlassian-hosted storage.

Who sees what

  • Every request checks the person's own Jira permissions. People see only work in projects they can browse.
  • Settings are for Jira admins only, checked on every request.
  • The Rovo agent runs as the person asking and sees only what they can browse. By default it only reads and runs what-if scenarios.
  • Loadline never changes your Jira issues. Its permissions are read-only, apart from reporting stored accounts to Atlassian.

Personal data

StoredWhy
Atlassian account IDsSettings history (including approvers added or removed), decision approvers and who added them, and who recorded a decision or note
Names of shared specialistsShowing who is a bottleneck: from their Atlassian account, or typed in by a Jira admin for someone without one. Copied into daily plan snapshots (90 days) and into a decision's capacity snapshot
Decision titles, rationale and notesThe ranking decision log, as people write them

No email addresses, passwords or credentials are stored. Hours from Jira time tracking are kept only as totals for each epic, never who logged them. Every week Loadline reports its stored accounts to Atlassian, erases closed accounts and refreshes changed names. Plan snapshots are kept for 90 days. When Loadline is uninstalled, Atlassian deletes its data.

The full details are in the privacy policy and the data processing addendum.
