Support SLA

The support Loadout Labs LLC provides for Loadline: how to reach us, when, and how fast we respond.

Version 1.0, effective November 4, 2026

How to get support

Raise a request in the Loadline Support portal, where anyone can sign up, follow their request and reply in one place. If you can't use the portal, email support@loadoutlabshq.com. Please include your Jira site address, what you expected and what happened instead, and the Jira issue keys or Loadline screens involved. Screenshots help. Never send passwords or API tokens; we will never ask for them.

This SLA applies to every Jira site with an active Loadline subscription or free trial from the Atlassian Marketplace.

Support hours

Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00 US Eastern time, excluding US federal public holidays. Requests received outside these hours are handled from the start of the next business day.

Response times

We aim to send a first response, from a person, within these times. It confirms we are working on your request and, where possible, gives a fix, a workaround or the next step.

SeverityWhat it meansFirst response
CriticalLoadline cannot be used at all on your site, for example no Loadline page loads for anyoneSame business day if received before 13:00 US Eastern time; otherwise the next business day
HighA major feature is broken with no workaround, for example syncs fail every timeNext business day
NormalQuestions, setup help, minor bugs, or problems with a workaroundWithin 2 business days
Feature requestA suggestion for something Loadline does not do yetAcknowledged within 5 business days

We set the severity when we first respond and change it if the impact turns out to be different. We keep you updated until the request is resolved, at least every 2 business days for Critical and High requests. Response times are targets for a first response, not a promise of when a fix will be released.

Security issues

Report suspected vulnerabilities to security@loadoutlabshq.com, not to the support address, and please don't share them publicly until they are fixed. We fix security vulnerabilities within the timeframes of Atlassian's Security Bug Fix Policy for Marketplace apps.

Availability

Loadline runs entirely on Atlassian's cloud and keeps its data in Atlassian-hosted storage, so its availability depends on Atlassian's platform, reported at status.atlassian.com. We do not offer a separate uptime commitment. If an Atlassian outage affects Loadline, we will help you confirm the cause and check that Loadline recovers once the platform does.

What this SLA does not cover

  • Problems caused by Atlassian's platform or products, including outages, which Atlassian supports.
  • Jira configuration and the data in Jira, such as missing dates, teams or statuses. Loadline's Help tab and data-quality flags explain what to change, and we are happy to advise.
  • Sites without an active Loadline subscription or trial.
  • Custom development, training or consulting.

We may update this SLA as our support grows. We will post changes here, and a change will not reduce the support for a subscription period you have already paid for.
